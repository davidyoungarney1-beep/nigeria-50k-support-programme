NEED CLARITY?

Frequently asked
questions.

Is the ₦50,000 payment guaranteed?

No. The amount shown is the stated programme amount, not a guarantee of payment. Funding, eligibility and disbursement must be confirmed by the authorised administrator.

Does CBN operate this website?

No. This is not a CBN website. The CBN link is provided only as an independent public reference.

Will I need to pay an application or activation fee?

Do not pay anyone to access a promised benefit unless a legitimate fee is clearly documented by the verified programme administrator. Be alert to impersonation and advance-fee scams.

Should I enter my BVN, NIN, password or bank details here?

No. This information page does not collect personal or banking information. Never share one-time passwords, card PINs or online banking passwords.

Where will application dates be announced?

Dates and application instructions should be published by the verified programme administrator. No application window is announced on this page.
